Grilled food · Colombia + Florida

Fire, protein, and the same table on both sides of the map.

Brasaland is a grilled-food restaurant chain. We cook sirloin, ribs, and chicken over the brasa, serve them with house sauce and arepas, and keep Brasa Points valid at every location in Colombia and Florida.

  • One chain in Colombia and Florida — miami-downtown, bogota-norte, and COL-01 through COL-10.
  • Sirloin, ribs, and chicken over the brasa, with house sauce and corn arepas.
  • Brasa Points work at every location. Florida stays in USD $; Colombia stays in COP $ — we never convert one into the other.

How a visit works

The table is the same in Colombia and Florida: grill, sauce, arepa, and Brasa Points that follow you to every location.

  1. 1

    Sit down at a Brasaland

    Walk into miami-downtown, bogota-norte, or any COL-01 through COL-10 location. Prices stay in that market’s currency.

  2. 2

    Eat from the grill

    Order sirloin, ribs, or chicken over the brasa. Add tropical salad, a corn arepa, and house sauce. Tell the server about allergies before the kitchen starts.

  3. 3

    Earn and redeem Brasa Points

    Earn 1 point for every 10 USD in Florida or every 10,000 COP in Colombia. Redeem from 15 points, in steps of 5. Five points equal 20 USD or 20,000 COP off the bill.

Brasa Points benefits

Tiers stack as you earn. Points stay valid while the account is active — at least one purchase every 12 months. Accounts are individual and not shared.

Bronze · 0–19 points

5% off drinks on Tuesdays.

Silver · 20–49 points

10% off the main dish, once a month.

Gold · 50+ points

15% permanent discount and early access to the seasonal menu.

Two markets, one grill

Florida

miami-downtown — USD market. Earn 1 Brasa Point for every 10 USD spent. Prices stay in USD $.

Colombia

bogota-norte plus COL-01 through COL-10 — COP market. Earn 1 Brasa Point for every 10,000 COP spent. Prices stay in COP $.

Supplier orders

Proteins weekly (48h). Produce twice weekly (24h). Beverages and packaging biweekly. Imported sauces monthly. Protein emergency orders carry an 8% surcharge.
