# Project Brief — Brasaland

## Business Context
Brasaland is a grilled-food restaurant chain founded in Medellin in 2008. It now operates 14 owned restaurants across Colombia and Florida (US), with around 115 employees and an estimated annual revenue near USD 6M.

## Why This Project Exists
Brasaland must scale consistent flavor, warm service, and fast kitchen operations across two countries without losing brand identity.

## Current Business Problems
- Operational fragmentation across 14 locations.
- Manual procurement through WhatsApp/phone and delayed price visibility.
- No central API, no unified data model, and low real-time visibility.
- Legacy digital channels (static website, outdated app, physical loyalty cards).
- Executive decisions rely on calls, PDFs, and intuition instead of live data.

## AI Engineering Objective
Build a progressive integrated digital platform that unifies operations, data, and AI capabilities in one monorepo:
- Public and internal user interfaces.
- Central API and core business entities.
- Real-time telemetry and transaction ingestion.
- Workflow automation.
- AI capabilities: prediction, personalization, and assistants.

## Key Constraints
- Multi-country operation: Colombia + Florida (US).
- Multi-currency operation: COP + USD.
- Potential bilingual support (Spanish/English).
- Heterogeneous POS integrations by country.
- Brand and operational consistency across locations.

## Success Criteria
Transition from delayed, intuition-based decisions to near real-time, data-assisted operations that can scale in both countries.
