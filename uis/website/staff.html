Telemetry

Events per day, api_error / user_login_failed counts, and authentication failure rate. Guest pages send page_view when this site is served by the API. Login attempts are recorded on /auth/login. Live query against Supabase when the host is reachable.

WebSocket chat over the Brasaland knowledge base: ordering, waste protocol, Brasa Points, and allergens.

Incident analysis

Upload or analyze a CSV, show summary metrics, and export results.
