Guest menu

What we cook

Brasaland is a grilled-food chain in Colombia and Florida. The guest menu disclosed here is six items: three from the grill, then tropical salad, corn arepa, and house sauce. This page does not list prices — Florida checks are in USD and Colombia checks are in COP, and we never convert one into the other.

From the grill

Sirloin, ribs, and chicken are the grilled mains. Silver Brasa Points members get 10% off a main dish once a month. Gold members (50+ points) get 15% off and early access to the seasonal menu; those seasonal dishes are not listed here.

Main · grilled

Grilled Sirloin

Lomo a la Brasa

Beef sirloin cooked over the brasa. This is the beef main on the disclosed guest menu, prepared with a marinade.

  • Cooked over the brasa.
  • Prepared with a marinade.
  • Counts as a main dish for Brasa Points.

Marinade contains soy. Declared gluten-free and dairy-free.

Main · grilled

Brasaland BBQ Ribs

With imported sauce

Ribs as a grilled main, finished with imported sauce. That sauce is listed separately from house sauce.

  • A grilled main.
  • Finished with imported sauce.
  • Counts as a main dish for Brasa Points.

Sauce contains soy. Ask a server about this dish every time if you need gluten-free food.

Main · grilled

Classic Grilled Chicken

Chicken main

Classic Grilled Chicken is the chicken main on the disclosed guest menu, cooked over the brasa.

  • Cooked over the brasa.
  • The chicken protein on the disclosed menu.
  • Counts as a main dish for Brasa Points.

Declared gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free.

Salad, side, and sauce

Tropical salad is a main. Corn arepa is the disclosed side. House sauce is its own item, separate from the imported sauce on the BBQ ribs.

Main

Tropical Salad

Cashew and feta

Tropical Salad is listed with the mains. The disclosed ingredients include cashew and feta cheese.

  • A main dish, not a side.
  • Disclosed with cashew and feta cheese.
  • Counts as a main dish for Brasa Points.

Contains nuts (cashew) and dairy (feta).

Side

Corn Arepa

The disclosed side

Corn arepa is the side on the disclosed guest menu, served with the grilled dishes.

  • Listed as a side, not a main.
  • Served with the grilled dishes.
  • Does not count as the Brasa Points main-dish discount.

Contains dairy and egg.

Sauce

House Sauce

Its own menu item

House sauce is listed separately from the proteins, the salad, and the arepa. It is not the imported sauce on the BBQ ribs.

  • Its own disclosed item.
  • Separate from the imported sauce on Brasaland BBQ Ribs.

Contains soy and sulfites.
