What we cook

Brasaland is a grilled-food chain. These six dishes are the ones the company discloses to guests. Allergen wording is copied from the menu allergen guide under INVIMA (Colombia) and FDA (United States) rules. This page does not list prices — Florida checks are in USD and Colombia checks are in COP, and we never convert one into the other.
