Grilled food · Colombia + Florida

Fire, protein, and the same table on both sides of the map.

Brasaland is a grilled-food restaurant chain. We cook sirloin, ribs, and chicken over the brasa, serve them with house sauce and arepas, and keep Brasa Points valid at every location in Colombia and Florida.

What we cook

Dishes and allergen facts below are taken from the Brasaland menu allergen guide. We disclose allergens under INVIMA (Colombia) and FDA (United States) rules.

Grilled Sirloin (Lomo a la Brasa)

Gluten-free and dairy-free. The marinade contains soy.

Brasaland BBQ Ribs

Contains soy. The imported sauce may contain trace peanuts. There is no certified gluten-free version of this dish.

Classic Grilled Chicken

Gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free.

Two markets, one program

Florida prices and redemptions are in USD. Colombia prices and redemptions are in COP. We never convert one into the other on this site.

Florida

miami-downtown — USD market. Earn 1 Brasa Point for every 10 USD spent.

Colombia

bogota-norte plus locations COL-01 through COL-10 — COP market. Earn 1 Brasa Point for every 10,000 COP spent.

Allergen protocol

Servers notify the kitchen before prep. Separate utensils and surfaces are used. We never claim zero cross-contamination risk.
