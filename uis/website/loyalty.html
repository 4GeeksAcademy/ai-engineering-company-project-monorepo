Loyalty

Brasa Points

For every 10,000 COP (or 10 USD in Florida) spent, you earn 1 point. Points do not expire while the account stays active (at least one purchase every 12 months).

Bronze · 0–19 points

5% off drinks on Tuesdays.

Silver · 20–49 points

10% off the main dish, once a month.

Gold · 50+ points

15% permanent discount and early access to the seasonal menu before the general public.

How redemption works

  • Redeem starting at 15 accumulated points, in increments of 5.
  • Every 5 points redeemed equal 20,000 COP (20 USD) of discount on the bill.
  • Points cannot be combined with other active monthly promotions.
  • The program exists on physical stamp cards (being phased out) and in the digital app.
  • A physical card can be transferred to the app only once, by presenting the completed card at any location.

Guest questions

Can points be used on delivery?

Yes. They apply the same way as in-store, as long as the order is placed through the app.

Can I share my account?

No. Every Brasa Points account is individual and non-transferable between people.

What if I move countries?

Points are valid at any Brasaland location in Colombia and Florida, at the day’s exchange rate.
